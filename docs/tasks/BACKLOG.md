### Attempt commit robustness (ATT — found by the owner 2026-10-19)

- [ ] **ATT-001: a stale schedule revision silently dropped the attempt, so the puzzle came straight back** — owner report: *"sometimes when i do a puzzle i have to repeat it again"*.
